Stage 2: Dialogue, Comprehension & Rules 15 Mins

Part A: Dialogue Role-Play (Mia & Noah's Science Fair Story)

Mia: Noah, you look so exhausted today! Are you okay?
Noah: Ugh, not really. If I had gone to bed sooner, I wouldn't be so tired right now! I stayed up till 1 AM playing online games. I wish I hadn't stayed up so late!
Mia: Oh no! And didn't we have our science project due this morning?
Noah: Yes! I shouldn't have procrastinated. If I had finished my poster board on Sunday, I would have had a clean, finished project. Losing points on presentation was really unfortunate.
Mia: If only you had called me, I would have helped you glue the charts! Also, did you eat breakfast?
Noah: No, I ran out the door and missed the bus! If I had taken the bus, I wouldn't have been late to homeroom. And if I had eaten breakfast, I wouldn't be starving right now!
Mia: Haha, don't worry. I brought two apple muffins. I wish you wouldn't stress so much[cite: 5]—here, take one! Let's treat today as a fresh start!
Noah: Thanks Mia! You saved my day. Next time, I promise to follow a better study routine[cite: 4]!

Part B: Click Cards to Master Grammar Patterns

I wish + Past Simple

Present Wish: Wanting something different right now (e.g., I wish I had more time).

I wish + Past Perfect

Past Regret: Looking back at what went wrong (e.g., I wish I hadn't stayed up late).

I shouldn't have + V3

Admitting you did the wrong action in the past (e.g., I shouldn't have argued).

3rd Conditional (Past)

If + had + V3, would have + V3 (Imagining an impossible changed past!)

Mixed Conditional

If + had + V3, would be (now) (A past choice changing how you feel right now!)

If only...

A more dramatic, emotional way to say "I wish" (e.g., If only you had called!)

Exercise 3: Situation & Grammar Sense Check (7 Questions)

15. Saying "I wish I had more time" expresses a regret about 10 years ago.

16. In 3rd conditional, we use had + past participle in the IF-clause to talk about the past.

17. "I shouldn't have eaten that third slice of cake" means you are glad you ate it[cite: 4].

18. "If only..." can be used just like "I wish..." to express deep desires or regrets.

19. "Quitting the school band was unfortunate" means you feel pleased that you quit[cite: 4].

20. "If I had studied more, I would have passed" means the speaker didn't study enough in reality.

21. It is better to have tried and learned than to never have tried at all!
