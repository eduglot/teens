Super Friends: Online ESL Adventure

Total Class Duration: 60 Minutes
1. Warm-up: Complete Friendship Trait Ranks 10 Mins

What is important in a friendship? Rate all the qualities from our worksheet list! (1 Star = Nice to have, 3 Stars = Absolutely essential!).

1. Loyalty (Stands by you through thick and thin)
2. Trustworthy (Keeps your deepest secrets safe)
3. Dependable (Always shows up when you need a hand)
4. Similar sense of humor (Laughs at the same funny jokes)
5. Age (Being close in age or in the same grade)
6. Appearance (Having cool sneakers or trendy style)
7. Wealth (Having the newest video games and toys)
8. Religion / Beliefs (Sharing similar core values)
9. Education level (Getting similar grades in school)
10. Politics / Fairness (Agreeing on class rules and elections)
11. Other (Kindness, sharing snacks, patience, etc.)
2. Presentation: Extended Dialogue & Vocabulary Cards 15 Mins

Part A: Extended Character Dialogue (Read aloud in pairs!)

Leo: Hey Maya! You look cheerful today. Did you and Sam finally sort out that huge argument from yesterday?
Maya: Yes, thank goodness! We sat together at lunch and decided to bury the hatchet. We had a silly falling out over who gets the lead part in the school play, but it really wasn't worth being upset.
Leo: I'm so relieved to hear that! You two go way back to nursery school, right?
Maya: We sure do! In fact, we hit it off the very first day we played in the sandbox together. Ever since then, everyone says we're practically joined at the hip!
Leo: Haha, that's completely true! Even the teachers joke that you two are like two peas in a pod because you dress alike, draw together, and finish each other's sentences.
Maya: Haha, we really do. After so many years, she knows me inside out. She knows when I'm feeling down before I even say a single word.
Leo: That is what a genuine BFF is for. It must feel great having someone who acts as a true shoulder to cry on whenever classes get stressful.
Maya: Definitely. A real friend stays with you through thick and thin. No matter what happens, I know she will always have my back.
Leo: And doesn't hurt that her older brother is the head hall monitor, right?
Maya: Haha, yep! It never hurts to have friends in high places when we need library passes!

Part B: Click Cards to Reveal Idiom Meanings

Two peas in a pod

Very similar in behavior, taste, or style.

BFF

Best Friends Forever.

Go way back

Have known each other for a very long time.

Joined at the hip

Always together and practically inseparable.

Know inside out

To know someone completely and thoroughly.

Shoulder to cry on

A supportive listener when you feel sad or upset.

Bury the hatchet

Stop arguing and agree to make peace.

Have a falling out

To have a serious fight or dispute.

Through thick & thin

Remaining loyal through easy times and hard times.

Have someone's back

Standing up to protect and support someone.

Friends in high places

Knowing leaders, teachers, or influential people.

Hit it off

To click and become friends immediately on meeting.
