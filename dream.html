Stage 2: Presentation & Character Dialogue 15 Mins

Part A: Dialogue Role-Play (Read aloud as Emma & Lucas!)

Emma: Hey Lucas! Did you finish writing down your bucket list for the new school year?
Lucas: I sure did! My dream is to build a working solar-powered robot. It’s a very ambitious project, but it's my top goal this term!
Emma: Whoa, that sounds amazing! Some kids said building a real robot in elementary school was just a pipe dream, but I know you can hit your target!
Lucas: Thanks Emma! I've been wanting to enter the regional science fair for two whole years[cite: 3]. What about you? What is on your bucket list?
Emma: Well, one day I hope to publish a fantasy graphic novel. And for this semester, I hope to start a weekend storybook creators' club!
Lucas: That sounds super cool! Please count me in as your very first member!
Emma: Awesome! Wait... you don't have an ulterior motive to make me draw robot blueprints for you, do you?
Lucas: Haha, no hidden motives at all! I wish I had joined your art group sooner so we could share big ideas and conquer our goals together!

Part B: Click Cards to Study Vocabulary & Desire Expressions

Pipe dream

An unrealistic dream that is almost impossible to happen.

Bucket list

A list of exciting achievements you want to do in life.

Ambitious

Having a strong desire to achieve great success.

Ulterior motive

A secret, hidden reason for doing something.

Goal / Target

A specific objective or result you work hard to achieve.

One day...

At an unspecified time in the future.

Count someone in

To include someone in an activity or group.

My dream is to...

Expressing your ultimate personal hope.

I wish I could...

Expressing a strong desire for something hard or impossible.

I've been wanting to...

Something you wanted for a long duration of time.

I wish I had done...

Expressing regret about past choices.

I pray / hope to...

Expressing a heartfelt, earnest hope for success.
